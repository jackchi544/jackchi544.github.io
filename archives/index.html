Research & Academic Works (科研与论文)

1. Research Project (科研项目)

Quantum Properties of Ultracold Atom-Molecule Conversion Systems

超冷原子分子转换系统的量子特性研究 (Provincial-Level Project 省级项目)

Project Summary:
This project focuses on the dissipative dynamics of ultracold atom-molecule conversion systems, investigating non-Markovian dynamics and quantum control mechanisms in many-body quantum systems under dissipative environments. Based on open quantum system theory, we construct Hamiltonians and master equations for diatomic-to-many-body systems, innovatively proposing a Bloch sphere model. Numerical simulations reveal self-trapping phase transition thresholds induced by particle loss and scaling effects of conversion rates.
项目概要： 本项目聚焦超冷原子分子转换系统耗散动力学，重点研究多体量子系统在耗散环境下的非马尔可夫动力学演化规律与量子调控机制。基于开放量子系统理论，构建双原子至多体量子系统的哈密顿量与主方程，创新提出布洛赫球模型。数值模拟揭示了粒子损失诱导的自陷相变阈值及转换率尺度效应。

Responsibilities (负责工作):

  • Numerical Simulation & Programming:
    Implemented 4th-order Runge-Kutta algorithms in MATLAB/Python to solve nonlinear differential equations, simulating particle evolution and Bloch sphere trajectories, replicating literature-reported dynamics (e.g., conversion efficiency decay under particle loss).
    数值模拟与编程实现： 用MATLAB/Python实现四阶龙格库塔算法，求解非线性微分方程组，模拟粒子数演化与布洛赫球轨迹，复现文献中的动力学曲线（如粒子损失下的转换效率衰减）。
  • Theoretical Modeling:
    Derived Hamiltonians/master equations for diatomic/triatomic systems and nonlinear Schrödinger equations, validated coupling strength (U) and energy gap (R), established evolution equations for normalized population difference (S) and relative phase (θ), and analyzed stable points/quantum phase transitions.
    理论推导与模型构建： 推导双原子/三体系统的哈密顿量、主方程及非线性薛定谔方程，验证耦合强度U、能量差R的合理性，建立多体系统归一化居群差S和相对相位θ的演化方程，分析稳定点分布与量子相变条件。

2. Academic Paper (学术论文)

《Design and Optimization of Artificial Reef Materials and Structures for Intelligent Ocean Ranching: A Neural Network and CFD Approach》

《智能海洋牧场人工鱼礁材料与结构优化设计：神经网络与CFD方法》

Paper Summary:
This study integrates BP neural networks, CFD, 3D printing, and behavioral analysis to develop a technical framework for artificial reef optimization, providing systematic solutions for sustainable ocean ranching.
论文概要： 本研究集成BP神经网络、计算流体力学（CFD）、3D打印技术及生物行为分析，构建了从材料配比预测、结构流体动力学分析到生态效应验证的完整技术体系，为海洋牧场可持续发展提供系统性解决方案。

Responsibilities (负责工作):

  • BP Neural Network Development:
    Applied Dropout regularization to prevent overfitting, optimized hyperparameters to predict fly ash composition.
    BP神经网络模型开发与优化： 引入Dropout正则化防止过拟合，优化超参数预测粉煤灰成分。
  • CFD Modeling:
    Designed 3D structures (e.g., flow-guiding plates, upwelling holes) using ANSYS Geometry and SolidWorks/UG, followed by meshing for hydrodynamic analysis.
    几何建模与网格划分： 使用ANSYS Geometry模块和SolidWorks/UG完成三维建模（如导流板曲面、上升流型通孔设计）。

3. Patent (专利)

《Intelligent Pesticide Formulation System: A Multi-Parameter Decision-Making and Environmental Sensing Approach》

《农药精准配比智能系统的设计与实现：基于多参数决策与环境感知的创新方法》

Patent Summary:
The patent proposes a rule-driven, modular pesticide formulation system integrating multi-parameter decision algorithms, real-time environmental sensing, and adaptive logic to resolve dilution errors, chemical incompatibility, and environmental inefficiency.
专利概要： 本专利提出了一种基于规则驱动和模块化架构的农药精准配比智能系统，通过多参数决策算法、实时环境监测和自适应计算逻辑的创新整合，攻克了传统农药配比中的稀释不当、药剂互斥和环境低效等难题。

Responsibilities (负责工作):

  • Algorithm Development:
    Built a fuzzy Petri net-based decision model for optimal pesticide matching in multi-factor coupling scenarios.
    算法研发与数学模型优化： 建立基于模糊Petri网的智能决策模型，实现多因子耦合条件下的最优农药匹配。
  • Embedded System Design:
    Developed a high-precision sensing system using orthogonal phase-locked amplification and switched-capacitor bandpass filters, optimizing SNR-power Pareto frontiers.
    嵌入式系统开发： 采用正交锁相放大技术结合开关电容带通滤波器，开发了高精度环境感知系统。

4. 课题设计 (课题设计)

《All-terrain Intelligent Loading/Unloading Transport Robot: Multidisciplinary Mechatronic System Integration》

《全地形智能装卸运输机器人：跨学科机电系统集成设计》

Project Summary

Executed cross-disciplinary integration for an autonomous all-terrain robot, focusing on mechanical structure, power transmission, steering suspension, human-machine interaction, drive power supply, and main control communication systems. The robot features a 775×480×480mm tracked chassis (35kg weight, 150kg load capacity), 24V DC motor drive (5KM/h max speed), and Raspberry Pi-based intelligent control system. Key innovations include an automatic pitch leveling system (±15° adjustment via PID control) and human-robot collaborative lifting mechanism, enabling stable operation on stairs and complex terrains.

项目概要
主导全地形智能装卸运输机器人跨学科机电系统集成设计，涵盖结构、动力、转向悬挂、人机交互、驱动电源及主控通信模块。机器人采用775×480×480mm履带底盘（自重35kg，负载150kg），24V直流电机驱动（最高时速5KM/h），基于树莓派实现智能控制。核心创新包括自动俯仰调平系统（PID算法实现±15°动态调节）和人机协同抬升机构，确保楼梯及复杂地形的稳定作业。

Responsibilities

1. Structural Design & Simulation
  • Conducted finite element analysis (FEA) for high-strength aluminum alloy frame, optimizing topology to withstand 150kg load and off-road impacts.
  • Designed modular rubber track with anti-slip lines for marble/cement stairs, integrating independent suspension with hydraulic dampers to reduce vibration by 40%.
    结构设计与仿真
  • 对高强度铝合金框架进行有限元分析，优化拓扑结构以承受150kg负载及越野冲击。
  • 设计模块化橡胶履带（防滑纹路适配大理石/水泥楼梯），集成独立悬挂与液压阻尼器，振动抑制效果达40%。
2. Power Transmission System
  • Selected Songyuan 60GA775 DC motor with 1:50 reducer (12.5Nm output torque), verifying dynamic load capacity via cyclic impact tests on 40° slopes.
  • Developed gear-track hybrid transmission, integrating anti-slip braking system to ensure 95% power efficiency on uneven terrain.
    动力传输系统
  • 选型松源60GA775直流电机（1:50减速比，12.5Nm输出扭矩），通过40°斜坡循环冲击测试验证动态负载能力。
  • 设计齿轮-履带混合传动机构，集成防滑制动系统，复杂地形动力传输效率达95%。
3. Steering & Suspension Systems
  • Engineered adaptive steering mechanism with MPU9250 sensor feedback, achieving real-time centroid offset compensation (≤15° pitch angle).
  • Simulated suspension dynamics via ADAMS, optimizing spring preload and damper parameters for 60kg-load stability on 15° slopes.
    转向悬挂系统
  • 设计基于MPU9250传感器反馈的自适应转向机构，实现重心偏移实时补偿（俯仰角≤15°）。
  • 利用ADAMS仿真悬挂动力学，优化弹簧预紧力与阻尼参数，确保60kg负载在15°斜坡的稳定性。
4. Human-Machine Interaction
  • Developed mobile APP (Python) for real-time monitoring of load status, battery level, and platform inclination (LiDAR + vision camera data fusion).
  • Integrated multi-sensor alarm system (vibration/temperature/ultrasonic), triggering LED alerts for anomalies (e.g., motor overheating).
    人机交互系统
  • 开发Python移动APP，实时监控负载状态、电量及平台倾角（激光雷达+视觉相机数据融合）。
  • 集成多传感器报警系统（振动/温度/超声波），异常情况（如电机过热）触发LED预警。
5. Drive Power Supply & Management
  • Designed 24V/20000mAh lithium battery system with BMS, implementing regenerative braking energy recovery (15% energy efficiency improvement).
  • Engineered motor drive circuit with overvoltage/overcurrent protection, achieving 2-hour continuous operation.
    驱动电源管理
  • 设计24V/20000mAh锂电池系统（含BMS），实现再生制动能量回收（能效提升15%）。
  • 研发带过压/过流保护的电机驱动电路，保障2小时连续作业。
6. Main Control & Communication
  • Architected Raspberry Pi 4B-based hierarchical control system, integrating MQTT protocol for 5km-range wireless communication (AOK-2628AP industrial bridge).
  • Optimized PID control algorithms (position/speed/steering) with multi-body dynamics simulation, achieving 0.5s response time for stair climbing.
    主控与通信系统
  • 构建基于树莓派4B的分层控制系统，集成MQTT协议实现5km无线通信（AOK-2628AP工业网桥）。
  • 结合多体动力学仿真优化PID控制算法（位置/速度/转向），楼梯攀爬响应时间达0.5秒。

Key Technologies

  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA), Topology Optimization, Multi-sensor Data Fusion
  • PID Control Algorithm, Regenerative Energy Systems, MQTT Communication Protocol
  • Raspberry Pi-Based Intelligent Control, Modular Tracked Chassis Design
