Honors and Awards（荣誉奖励）

Second Prize of the 9th “Internet Plus” Competition at the school level
（第九届“互联网+”大赛校二等奖） - 12/2022 - 08/2023
Use SQL Server/MySQL to create a database of “rice products” in Xiaofang Village, Panjin, to store information such as different varieties, specifications, and packaging, and discover the hot and slow-selling products in the sales of agricultural products.Use Visual Studio Code to write the front-end code of the e-commerce platform, and adopt HTML to design web pages and wechat mini-programs (Xiaofangcun Rice).Display pictures, names, prices, detailed introductions and other information of rice;Use CSS style sheets to layout the page, achieve a reasonable arrangement of product images and text, and enhance the visual effect of the page.Use SPSS software to collect a large amount of data through channels such as market research, user questionnaires, and sales records, such as the influence degree of various factors like consumer purchasing behavior, preferences, agricultural product sales volume, and price fluctuations on sales volume, to make sales predictions.
运用 SQL Server / MySQL 创建盘锦小房村“大米产品”数据库，存储不同品种、规格、包装等信息，发现农产品销售中的热点产品和滞销产品。使用 Visual Studio Code 编写电商平台前端代码，采用 HTML设计网页和微信小程序（小房村大米）。展示大米的图片、名称、价格、详细介绍等信息；使用CSS样式表对页面进行布局，实现产品图片和文字的合理排列，提高页面的视觉效果。使用SPSS软件通过市场调研、用户问卷、销售记录等渠道收集大量数据，如消费者购买行为、偏好、农产品销售量、价格波动等各因素对销售量的影响程度进行销售预测。

我运用贪心算法和模拟退火算法解决城市轨道交通系统的列车时刻表优化问题。
Third Prize in the 6th “Paper Bridge Load-Bearing Capacity” Design Competition
（第六届“纸桥承重”设 计大赛三等奖） - 05/2023 - 06/2023
It reflects that in engineering practice, I can flexibly apply the professional knowledge I have learned (such as material mechanics, structural mechanics, etc.) to the actual design and production of paper bridge models.This competition experience demonstrated that in my freshman year, I was capable of using software (such as SolidWorks) for modeling and finite element analysis, and was able to conduct research on some structural designs and mechanical property optimizations.
体现了我在工程实践中，能将所学专业知识（如材料力学、结构力学等）灵活运用到实际的纸桥模型设计与制作中。而这个比赛经验展示了我在大一的时候能够有能力运用软件（如 SolidWorks）进行建模和有限元分析，能够对一些结构设计和力学性能优化进行研究。
First Prize in the provincial physics academic competition
（物理学术竞赛省一等奖） - 09/2022 - 12/2023
In this competition, I described the principle of the “Rotating pancake” phenomenon: It involves the description of physical principles such as the Coriolis force, fluid dynamics, conservation of angular momentum, etc. It is necessary to observe the movement direction and trajectory of the sphere and record experimental data, such as the rotational speed of the container, the radius of the container, the diameter of the sphere, etc.Based on the experimental data, analyze the relationship between the movement direction of the sphere and the relevant parameters to verify the effect of the Coriolis force.
我在这个比赛描述了“Rotating pancake”现象的原理：涉及科里奥利力、流体动力学、角动量守恒以及等物理原理的描述，需要观察球体的运动方向和轨迹，并记录下实验数据，如容器旋转速度、容器半径、球体直径等。根据实验数据，分析球体运动方向与相关参数之间的关系，验证科里奥利力的作用。
Third Prize in the Liaoning Region of the National College Students’ Mathematical Modeling Contest
（全国大学生数学建模竞赛辽宁赛区三等奖） - 08/2023 - 09/2023
The digital elevation model (DEM) of the seabed topography is constructed by using the greedy algorithm and the simulated annealing algorithm.
Through DEM, the seabed topographic features of the sea area to be measured can be intuitively understood, including slope, depth variation, etc., and the total length of the survey line, the percentage of the missed sea area in the total sea area to be measured, and the total length of the part with an overlap rate exceeding 20% can be calculated.
采用贪心算法和模拟退火算法构建海底地形的数字高程模型（DEM）。通过DEM可以直观地了解待测海域的海底地形特征，包括坡度、深度变化等并计算测线的总长度、漏测海区占总待测海域面积的百分比以及重叠率超过20%部分的总长度。

Third Prize of the 20th “Climbing Cup” Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition of Dalian University of Technology
（大连理工大学第二十届“攀登杯”创新创业竞赛三等奖） - 01/2024 - 04/2024
Focusing on the efficiency improvement and intelligent operation and maintenance technology development of distributed photovoltaic power generation systems, in terms of photovoltaic power generation efficiency calculation, the project adopts the random forest model to accurately predict the power generation of photovoltaic panels.By collecting a large amount of data, including key parameters such as light intensity and environmental temperature, and applying the reverse operation technology of power generation, combined with the Bayesian model and the Markov chain Monte Carlo method, the faults of photovoltaic systems are precisely analyzed.And introduce LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory Network) technology for data filling.
聚焦于分布式光伏发电系统的效率提升与智能运维技术开发，在光伏发电效率计算方面，项目采用随机森林模型对光伏板发电量进行精准预测。通过收集大量数据，包括光照强度、环境温度等关键参数，运用发电量逆运算技术，结合贝叶斯模型和马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛法，对光伏系统的故障进行精准分析。并引入 LSTM（长短期记忆网络）技术进行数据填补。
The school promotion for the bipedal race of the China Engineering Robot Competition
（中国工程机器人大赛双足竞步赛校推） - 03/2024 - 09/2024
Focusing on the cross-legged race robot, it is required to design the PCL program development control system by oneself, which involves professional knowledge in multiple aspects such as robotics, control theory, and programming technology.PCL (Point Cloud Library) is used in the field of robotics to process three-dimensional point cloud data and is crucial for the environmental perception, path planning, etc. of robots.The mechanical structure design of the robot is carried out by using CAD software such as SolidWorks to ensure that the hardware of the robot can meet the functional requirements of the control system and guarantee its stability and reliability on the track.
聚焦于交叉足竞步机器人，要求自己设计 PCL 程序开发控制系统，这涉及到机器人学、控制理论、编程技术等多方面的专业知识。我运用 CAD 软件如 SolidWorks 进行机器人的机械结构设计，确保机器人的硬件能够满足控制系统的功能要求并保证其在赛道上的稳定性和可靠性。
Second prize in the provincial physics experiment competition
（物理实验竞赛省二等奖） - 03/2024 - 05/2024
In the ultrasonic experiment, I skillfully operated professional instruments such as oscilloscopes, sound wave transmitters, and data recording devices, and precisely completed a series of complex experimental operations including the measurement of the propagation characteristics of ultrasonic waves in different solid media and the analysis of attenuation characteristics.A large amount of data was generated during the experiment, which requires the use of professional data analysis software (such as Origin, MATLAB, etc.) for processing and analysis.Through these software, I conducted in-depth mining of data such as the propagation speed, amplitude, and attenuation characteristics of ultrasonic waves, drew directivity graphs, attenuation curves, etc, and used statistical methods to conduct error analysis and fitting of the data. I also actively explored innovative methods and techniques, such as trying different coupling agents, optimizing probe designs, and improving experimental devices.
在超声波实验中，我熟练操作了示波器、声波发射器、数据记录设备等专业仪器，精准地完成了超声波在不同固体介质中的传播特性测量、衰减特性分析等一系列复杂实验操作。实验过程中产生了大量的数据，需要运用专业的数据分析软件（如 Origin、 MATLAB 等）进行处理和分析。我通过这些软件对超声波传播速度、振幅、衰减特性等数据进行了深入挖掘，绘制了指向性图、衰减曲线等），并运用统计学方法对数据进行误差分析和拟合，积极探索创新的方法和技术，例如尝试不同的耦合剂、优化探头设计、改进实验装置等。
